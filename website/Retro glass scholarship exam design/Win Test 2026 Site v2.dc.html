Our Guiding Belief

A child's future should be built around their abilities, not our expectations

Parents naturally aspire to a bright future for their children. Yet a course or a career is often chosen from our own expectations, or from prevailing social trends, long before anyone has understood the child's aptitude, interests and strengths.

Win Test 2026 exists to close that gap. It is a comprehensive academic assessment that helps students identify their strengths, recognise their learning gaps and understand their own potential — while recognising and rewarding excellence.

The Five Areas of Assessment

A single objective paper of two hours. English medium follows the CBSE syllabus; Malayalam medium follows the Kerala syllabus — always for the student's own class.

English 15 MARKS

Comprehension, grammar and clarity of expression.

Science 20 MARKS

Conceptual understanding, observation and curiosity.

Mathematics 20 MARKS

Numerical fluency, logic and problem solving.

General Knowledge 15 MARKS

Awareness of the nation and the wider world.


Mental Ability 30 MARKS

Reasoning, patterns and spatial thinking — the most heavily weighted area, and the one that cannot be crammed.

Frequently Asked

Is this a competitive entrance examination?

No. It is an assessment with scholarships attached; no student is excluded from anything on its basis.

In which medium may my child write?

English or Malayalam. The English paper follows the CBSE syllabus and the Malayalam paper the Kerala syllabus.

Is coaching necessary?

No. The paper remains within the student's own class syllabus, and Mental Ability cannot be crammed.

What if my school is not registered?

Ask your class teacher to write to us. Onboarding a school takes only a few days.

The Curriculum & Scheme of Examination

The paper is set on the syllabus of the student's own class and does not extend above grade level. For the English medium it follows the CBSE syllabus; for the Malayalam medium, the Kerala syllabus. Mental Ability alone is not syllabus-bound: it examines reasoning, patterns and spatial thinking.

CBSE syllabus
Malayalam Medium

Scheme by class group

Group A
LKG – Class II

Picture-based and orally styled questions: letters and sounds, counting and shapes, everyday awareness and simple odd-one-out reasoning.

Group B
Class III – V

Reading comprehension and the basics of grammar, the four operations and fractions, environmental science, national and general awareness, series and patterns.

Group C
Class VI – VIII

Comprehension and usage, integers, ratio and geometry, the fundamentals of physics, chemistry and biology, current affairs, coding-decoding and logical reasoning.

Group D
Class IX – X

Advanced comprehension, algebra, trigonometry and mensuration, applied science, civics and world affairs, analytical reasoning and data interpretation.

Group E
Class XI – XII

A stream-neutral core: language proficiency, quantitative aptitude, the fundamentals of science, contemporary affairs, and higher-order reasoning and aptitude.
